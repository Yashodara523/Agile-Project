# 2. Stakeholders and Communication Plan

## 2.1 Stakeholder Register

| Stakeholder | Role in project | Interest | Influence | Engagement strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Academic supervisor | Sponsor, approves scope and final report | High | High | Manage closely: sprint review, one-page report every two weeks |
| Hearing-impaired students | End users, test participants | High | Medium | Involve: interviews in Sprint 1, usability tests in Sprint 4 |
| Teachers of hearing-impaired students | Content authors, test participants | High | Medium | Involve: interviews, teacher-tool feedback in Sprints 2 to 3 |
| Parents / guardians | Consent for minors | Medium | Medium | Keep informed: consent forms, plain-language summary |
| School / institution | Hosts testing sessions | Medium | High | Keep satisfied: scheduling, approvals |
| Sign-language interpreter | Reviews sign-language content quality | Medium | Low | Consult: content review at the end of Sprint 2 |
| Development team | Builds product | High | High | Collaborate daily |
| Academic examiners / future readers | Assess final report | Medium | Medium | Keep informed: final report |

### Power / Interest Grid

| | Low interest | High interest |
|---|---|---|
| **High influence** | School / institution (keep satisfied) | Supervisor, development team (manage closely) |
| **Low influence** | Examiners (monitor) | Students, teachers, interpreter, parents (keep informed and involved) |

## 2.2 Communication Plan

Since the product serves hearing-impaired users, the team adopted **written and visual-first communication** as a working standard. This also made meetings easier to follow and record.

| Communication | Purpose | Frequency | Format / Channel | Owner | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily stand-up | Progress, plan, blockers | Daily, 15 min | Video call with live captions; written notes posted in chat | PM | Team |
| Sprint planning | Agree sprint goal and scope | Start of each sprint, 90 min | Call plus shared board | PM, BA | Team |
| Backlog refinement | Clarify and size stories | Mid-sprint, 60 min | Call plus board | BA | Team |
| Sprint review | Demo working software, get feedback | End of sprint, 60 min | Live demo, recorded | PM | Team, supervisor, invited users |
| Sprint retrospective | Improve how we work | End of sprint, 45 min | Online whiteboard | PM | Team |
| Supervisor status report | Progress, risks, decisions needed | Every 2 weeks | One-page PDF plus short meeting | PM | Supervisor |
| Risk review | Update risk register | Weekly, 15 min (inside stand-up) | Risk register | PM | Team |
| User feedback sessions | Validate designs and features | Sprint 1, 3, 4 | In-person or video with interpreter | BA, UX | Users |
| Ad-hoc questions | Quick clarification | As needed | Team chat | Everyone | Team |

## 2.3 Team Working Agreements

1. Every verbal decision is written in the team chat or on the board within the same day.
2. Meetings use live captions and a shared notes document.
3. Questions are asked in writing first; calls are for discussion, not for hidden decisions.
4. Blockers are raised immediately, not held until stand-up.
5. Work in progress is limited: no more than 2 items in progress per person.
6. Pull requests are reviewed within one working day.

## 2.4 Escalation Path

| Level | Who | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team member to PM | Blocker not solved in 1 day |
| 2 | PM to supervisor | Risk of missing a sprint goal or milestone, scope conflict, access to users blocked |
